Henni

A voxel pet. After 30 seconds on the page he walks in through a door and follows your cursor through his day. Rest the cursor and he sits down to code, record a podcast or mix a drink; click him to skip ahead.

Too impatient? The lab jumps to any part of his day and swaps his colourways.
